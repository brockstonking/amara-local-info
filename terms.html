AMARA Local use and terms.

AMARA Local is a privately operated personal assistant. These pages identify its Google integration; they do not offer a public hosted service, accounts or subscriptions.

Operation

The owner controls the computer, enabled sources, observation settings, named enrollment, Google connections and backups. Access to another person's information or an external service requires the appropriate authorization. Face recognition supplies descriptive context and is not an authentication or account-permission mechanism.

Generated output

Model-generated descriptions, summaries, suggestions and face matches can be mistaken. The interface distinguishes retained source material from tentative interpretation. The operator should review consequential actions and maintain independent backups of original documents.

Components and external services

Third-party software and model components remain subject to their respective licenses. Google account and API use remain subject to Google's applicable terms and policies. This website does not change those agreements or provide an uptime, accuracy or recovery guarantee.

Data handling

The privacy policy describes the application's local processing and Google data handling. The operator controls enabling features and retaining, correcting or removing information.
